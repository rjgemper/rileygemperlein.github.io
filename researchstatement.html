Research Statement

My research agenda focuses on heavy metal exposure and the underpinnings of altered reproductive outcomes. Through mixed-effect modeling I seek to investigate the extent to which metal concentration in groundwater modifies methylation processes, underscoring the quantitative biological shifts that delineate the etiology of birth outcomes. With those biochemical deltas as my foundation, I strive to design community-driven drinking water interventions targeting global disparities in altered gestational processes, highlighting the manner through which environmental epidemiology girds reproductive health.
